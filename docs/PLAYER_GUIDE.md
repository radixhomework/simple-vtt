# Player Guide — RHW Simple VTT

Welcome! This guide covers everything a **player** can do. Your game master
(admin) controls the maps, fog of war and monsters — you just play.

## Signing in

1. Open the application address given by your GM.
2. Enter the username and password your GM created for you.
3. Sessions last **12 hours** — after that you simply sign in again.

### Changing your password

On the home page, click the **🔑** button in the top-right corner, fill in
your current password and the new one twice, then **Save**.

## Joining a table

The home page lists the tables you can join. Click **Join** on a card to
enter the map. Use **← VTT** (top-left) to come back.

## Moving around the map

| Action              | Mouse                     | Touch / Apple Pencil              |
|---------------------|---------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Move your token     | Left-click and drag it    | One finger (or pencil) on it      |
| Pan the map         | Right-click and drag      | One finger on empty space         |
| Zoom                | Scroll wheel              | Two fingers: pinch or drag        |

- You can only drag **your own tokens** (unless the GM opened movement for
  everyone). Tokens never walk through walls or closed doors.
- A quick **tap / click** on a token selects it.
- Hidden enemies are truly invisible to you — their position is not even
  sent to your browser.

## Measuring distances

The toolbar (top of the screen) offers measurement tools:

- **╱ line** — straight distance
- **◯ circle** — area radius
- **▭ square** — rectangular area
- **◤ cone** — 90° cone

Distances display in real units (feet or metres — the GM configures how big
a grid square is). The GM may also draw a measurement that appears on
every player's screen.

## Chat

If the GM has chat enabled, the chat box sits at the bottom-left of the map.
Type your message and press **Enter**.

## Music

The music panel (**🎵** button) shows the current track and the queue:

- **⏮ ▶ ⏸ ⏭** — previous, play/pause, next (works for any player)
- **↑ / ↓** on a track — reorder the queue
- **Volume slider** — adjusts the volume **in your browser only**; it never
  changes what others hear

Playback is synchronised: when someone presses next, everyone's browser
follows. If your browser blocks audio until you interact, click anywhere on
the page once.

## Full screen

Press **⛶** to hide every menu and use the whole screen. **Esc** brings the
menus back.

## Keyboard shortcuts (on the map)

`S` select · `L` line · `C` circle · `Q` square · `N` cone · `Esc` deselect ·
`Delete` is reserved for the GM.

## Troubleshooting

- **The map is empty / tokens are missing** — the GM may have hidden them or
  your session expired; reload and sign in again.
- **I can't move a token** — it probably isn't yours, or the GM restricted
  movement to owned tokens.
- **No sound** — check the volume slider, then click anywhere on the page
  (browser autoplay policy).

## Doors and windows

Depending on the permissions the admin granted, you may click **doors**
and **windows** to open and close them yourself. Closed windows let you
*see* through them, but nobody can walk through a closed window.
